# Adoption pack — Weekly coverage digest

**The problem:** The team reads clips for ninety minutes on Monday and leaves with a different story about the week than the one the data supports.

**Owner:** Head of Communications · **Sponsor:** Director
**Trigger:** the scheduler runs at 06:00 Monday; the digest is on the shared drive before 07:00
**Format:** one page: five items that matter, why each matters, and who else carried the story

## Where it sits

- **Ritual:** Monday coverage review (weekly, Monday 09:00)
- **Moment:** first item, before anyone opens a browser
- **In the room:** Head of Communications, media officer, programme lead
- **First artefact:** the output is read before anything else in that ritual

## First weeks

### Week 1 — shadow (2026-11-16)
Run it beside the existing process. Nobody consumes the output yet; the owner compares it to what the team would have said anyway.

### Week 2 — live (2026-11-23)
The output becomes the first artefact in the ritual. The old artefact is withdrawn the same day, not kept “for a while”.

### Review — week 4 (2026-12-14)
Measure review time to reach a decision against target, in the same room, and decide keep, change or stop.

## How we will know

- **Metric:** review time to reach a decision
- **Target:** 90 minutes down to 30, within four weeks
- **Source:** meeting start and end time, logged by the chair
- **Review:** 2026-12-14

## What we stop doing

- printing the raw clips list for the meeting
- the separate spreadsheet where each officer kept their own count

## What will go wrong

- **Nobody opens it** — the digest is the first agenda item; the chair opens it on screen
- **The team trusts the score more than the story** — every item prints its reasons; one story is read in full each week
- **It becomes a second process** — the old clips list is withdrawn in week two, same day as go-live

## If it fails

Revert to the manual list for two weeks, then diagnose whether the failure was the tool or the ritual.

---

_A tool is adopted when the old artefact is gone. If both run in parallel for a month, this was a demo._
